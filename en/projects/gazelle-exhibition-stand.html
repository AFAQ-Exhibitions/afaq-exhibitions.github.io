The Challenge

Turn a standard shell-scheme plot into a clear branded wall for GAZELLE: product shelving that reads from the aisle and a reception point for visitors.

Design Concept

A white wall repeats the GAZELLE name along its length, with the brand blue on the shelf edges and the counter front.

Execution

Built and installed by AFAQ on site: slatted wall panels with floating display shelves, a branded reception counter and display tables, ready for the opening day.

Materials & Finishes

  • White wall panels with branded lettering
  • Floating shelves with blue edge trim
  • Branded reception counter
