We design stands and build them

AFAQ Exhibitions is an exhibition design and build studio based in Damascus. One team takes your stand from the first sketch to handover in the hall.

AFAQ means “horizons”

Afaq is the Arabic word for horizons. In an exhibition hall, the horizon is the line of stands a visitor sees from the aisle. Our job is to make yours the one they walk toward.

Why we work this way

At an exhibition you have a few days, and each visitor gives you a few seconds. A stand has to explain who you are before anyone speaks. So we start from what a visitor should understand at a glance, then design and build around that.

“Stands that are carefully designed and built as drawn.”

Mission

To design and build stands that help companies meet the people they came to the exhibition to meet.

Vision

To be the exhibition partner that companies in the region call first.

Our Philosophy

“A stand that looks good but says nothing has not done its job. We judge our work by whether visitors understand you and stop.”

What we care about

Clarity
You always know what is happening and what comes next.
Precision
We measure, check and build to the drawing.
Craftsmanship
Joints, edges and finishes are done properly, including the ones visitors won’t notice.
Presence
A stand should be seen and understood from across the hall.
Partnership
We work with your team and tell you early when something needs a decision.

How we work

  • One team

    The people who design your stand are the people who build it, so the stand matches the drawing.

  • A written brief

    Space, budget, dates and scope are agreed in writing before design starts.

  • You see it first

    You approve the 3D views and technical drawings before anything is built.

  • Checked at every stage

    We check the work at each stage, not only at handover.

  • Ready on time

    The stand is installed and tested before the hall opens.

  • With you during the show

    Our team is on call through the show days.
